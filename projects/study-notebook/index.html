THE PROJECT

Personal Study Notebook

A personal language-study notebook that brings notes, playlists, flashcard-style quizzes and media attachments into one place.

Why I built it

I built it to keep my language-study notes and revision materials in one place.

My contribution

I built the React study interface and connected Supabase authentication, note storage and media uploads. The notebook includes dedicated Portuguese and Mandarin study spaces.

INSIDE THE EXPERIENCE

How it comes together.

  1. 01Capture notes
  2. 02Build a playlist
  3. 03Review & recall
01

Organize study material

Create and edit notes, separate them by study language, and group them into playlists.

02

Practice recall

Review notes with flashcard-style quizzes that reveal the answer when you are ready.

03

Keep more than text

Attach and play audio or video. A separate plain-text notebook saves changes automatically.
